## Possible Causes of Absence

1. **Corpus filtering** — search queries used narrow flood/water keywords; foundational
   methods papers (Nash-Sutcliffe, SCS curve number) were excluded even though every
   hydrological paper cites them.

2. **Title-keyword recall limits** — journal-level exclusions (e.g. Nature Climate Change,
   JGR-Atmospheres) removed high-impact papers outside the direct flood-mapping domain.

3. **Missing ingestion** — some papers have DOIs in references.parquet but their PDFs
   were never downloaded; GROBID therefore never processed them.

4. **Topic ontology gaps** — the current ontology focuses on flood mapping / SAR /
   optical remote sensing; papers primarily about climate, hydrology theory, or general
   EO infrastructure are under-represented.

5. **Paywalled or retracted papers** — a small fraction may be inaccessible via open
   channels; absent from GROBID output and therefore from the corpus.
